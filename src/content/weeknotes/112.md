## End of the Hockey Season

I-League ended with our consolation game Monday night. We ended up winning 4-3 with some strong play on both offense and defense. My play in net wasn't bad, but I wanted 2 of the 3 goals I allowed back.

The Bombers made the playoffs and we won the quarterfinals game 3-2 in the fading seconds. We hoped we could take the momentum of that into Wednesday's game against the top-seeded team, but sadly lost 5-2. We played hard and we kept ourselves in it, but couldn't claw back the deficit. It was a good season and I feel like we made some strides as a team this season that we can hopefully carry over to next season.

## Lots of Runs

I've been again putting off lifting weights, and instead have put on my running shoes. Even with the three hockey games, I ended up running 20 miles this week. Keeps the blood flowing.

## Dark Ink, Chuck Wendig & Clay McLeod Chapman

On Friday Night, Lauren and I drove out to Doylestown for the start of the Dark Ink events put on by the [Doylestown Bookshop](https://doylestownbookshop.com/). Chuck Wendig and Clay McLeod Chapman were there for the release of their new books. Chuck just released [The Calamities](https://doylestownbookshop.com/book/9798217091812), and Clay just released [Devil Inside](https://doylestownbookshop.com/book/9780778306214).

I've been a fan of Chuck Wendig's after reading [Black River Orchard](https://doylestownbookshop.com/book/9780593158760) in 2023, and his two books, [Wanderers](https://doylestownbookshop.com/book/9780399182129) and [Wayward](https://doylestownbookshop.com/book/9780593158791) are among my favorites. I saw him last year with Clay for the release of [The Staircase in the Woods](https://doylestownbookshop.com/book/9780593156568) and the two of them were hilarious. It actually got me to pick up a [few](https://doylestownbookshop.com/book/9781683693062) [of](https://doylestownbookshop.com/book/9781683693789) [Clay's](https://doylestownbookshop.com/book/9781683693956) [books](https://bookshop.org/p/books/acquired-taste-clay-mcleod-chapman/2d97fa26f163e8c5), and I enjoyed them as well.

So when both were coming back for a dual book release event, we got tickets and made a date night out of it. Dinner at a bar in Doylestown followed by some book shopping at an independent book store, then a fun author event...count me in! Chuck and Clay were funny and interesting and now I have several more of their books...and they're signed. Fun way to end the work week.
