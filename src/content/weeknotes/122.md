## Cooking

### Jalapeño Popper Chicken Chili Soup

It's an older recipe I have, but with the weather turning a little more chilly, it's soup season. I'm not sure where I found it as I've been making it for over a decade, but it's delicious.

#### Ingredients

- Olive Oil
- 1 ½ cups Chicken Broth
- 4 Jalapenos, seeded & diced
- 3 Cloves Garlic, minced
- 1 tsp. Oregano
- 14.5 oz. Can Petite Diced Tomatoes
- 15 oz. Can Cannellini Beans
- 1 Small Onion, diced
- 1 ½ lb. Chicken Breast, cubed
- Salt & Pepper
- 8 oz. Can Corn
- 1 tsp. Cumin
- 1 Red Bell Pepper, diced
- 2 tsp. Chili Powder
- 8 oz. Cream Cheese
- Shredded Mexican Cheese

#### Directions

1. In a large, heavy-bottomed saucepan (dutch oven), heat 2 tbsp oil over medium heat and sauté onion, both types of peppers and garlic until soft and tender 
2. Season the chicken with salt and pepper
3. Add another tbsp oil to pan and lightly brown chicken on all sides
4. Season with chile/chili powder, cumin, and oregano
5. Add tomatoes, chicken broth, beans, and corn
6. Bring to a simmer and cook 30 minutes, seasoning as needed
7. Add cream cheese and stir until completely melted; remove from heat
8. Serve with crumbled bacon and cheese
