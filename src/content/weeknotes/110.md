## More Smoke Detector Fun

I replaced all the smoke detectors in January as the old ones were at least 10 years old. The ones I got kept randomly going off and for the better part of two months we kept trying to figure out why. We never did. In April, I replaced them again, this time with ionization smoke detectors, and they've been fine since...until Thursday. Thursday the detector went off in the morning. As best as we can tell, the extreme humiditiy was setting it off. Ionization ones are apparently susceptible to humiditiy and false alarms. So we're replacing that one again and hoping for the best. The attic does get very hot and humid in the summer, but there's only so much we can do about it. It's just frustrating trying to figure out a way to make sure we get notified when there *is* a problem without getting blasted by false alarms.
