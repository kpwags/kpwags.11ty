## Recipes

### Mexican Street Corn Bowl

I didn't have a recipe for this one. While we were in Cape May last week, my wife had something like this for dinner at a restaurant. She liked it so I decided to try my hand at a facsimile. It turned out pretty well. Here's a rough recipe for it.

#### Ingredients

- 1-1.5 lbs. Boneless Skinless Chicken Thighs
- Chipotle Aoli (to taste)
- (2) 15 oz. cans Corn, drained
- (1) 15 oz. can Black Beans, drained
- Vegetable Oil
- Mexican Rice package
- Cojita Cheese (to taste)
- Salt & Pepper
- (2) Avocados, diced
- Pico de Gallo (to taste)

#### Directions

1. Make Mexican Rice per package instructions
2. Season chicken thighs with salt & pepper and bake for 25 minutes at 375ºF or until internal temperature reaches 165ºF. Cut into bite size pieces and set aside.
3. In a sauce pan, heat the black beans on medium-low heat to get them warm
4. In a pan, heat 1-2 tbsp. vegetable oil. Once heated, add corn and sauté for 4-6 minutes until warm and cooked through.
5. In a large pan add 1 tbsp. of vegetable oil. Add rice, corn, black beans, chicken, chipotle aoli, and cojita cheese. Stir to combine and warm everything.
6. Divide and serve in bowls. Garnish with avocado and pico.

Makes about 6 servings.
