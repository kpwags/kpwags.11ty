## Recovering

I spent the early part of the week still taking it easy recovering from this cold. Thankfully I was definitely on the upswing, but man, it was not fun.

## Good Sunroom Progress

Despite not feeling perfect, all week I was able to make good progress on the sunroom. As it stands now, I have two coats of paint left, cutting and attaching the baseboard covers, and then replacing the ceiling fan. That's it, it will then be done. Then it's just cleanup and finding furniture for it. I'm excited. It's been several years in the making and I can finally see the finish line.

## Menzingers Concert

Thankfully I was feeling better for this. Friday, we went into Philly for The Menzingers concert. We saw Five Hundred Bucks, Mercy Union, Hot Water Music, and The Menzingers. Weakened Friends were also playing, but we had to miss them as I really wanted to see Mercy Union and they were playing on different stages with overlapping times. Mercy Union and Hot Water Music were awesome. I'd been wanting to see Hot Water Music for some time now, and finally got the chance.

The Menzingers set was a disappointment. The sound for them was all off. I'm not sure what was going on with the soundboard, but the audio was grating and the melody of their songs was nearly non-existent. It kind of felt like everything was just turned up too loud. With several songs, I only knew what song they were playing by the lyrics. Having seen them 12 times now, this was the first time I've ever been disappointed by their set, so I'll chalk it up as a fluke. If Hot Water Music hadn't sounded fine before them, I'd blame the venue, but in this case, it was them.
