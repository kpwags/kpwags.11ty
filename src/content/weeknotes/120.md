## The Week Leading Up to Vacation is Always Long

Given that we were finally going on a vacation, the week leading up to it felt long and drawn out. Every day felt longer than it should. I also didn't want to pick up any big tickets at work as to not have to remember exactly where I was in the development process. It tends to be easier to get back into the swing of things when I return to work.

## Vacation

My wife and I spent a week in Cape May, NJ to celebrate our anniversary. From our usual Cape May trips, it was much more low-key. We still went to a few wineries and breweries, but for the most part we just hung around the hotel and relaxed. The weather was perfect so we spent a lot of time on the hotel's veranda overlooking the ocean. For some it might feel like a "waste" of a vacation, but for us, it was perfect.

## Changing My Doodling Tooling

For almost the last year I've been trying to draw or doodle every day. I'd been doing it with my iPad and Procreate. I recently decided to try my hand at switching to a LEUCHTTURM1917 sketch book and my fountain pen. It feels a little different, especially without an eraser that I've enjoyed. I kind of enjoy the rough edges, the mistakes, and the final results.
