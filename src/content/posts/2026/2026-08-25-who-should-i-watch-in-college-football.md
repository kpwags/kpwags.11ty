To go along with my delve back into live sports, if I were going to start watching college football, who should I watch?
<!-- excerpt -->

I went to Drexel University who didn't have a football team. I had no college team to cheer on. I grew up rooting for Lehigh University football as my dad went there. I went to several games as a kid cheering them on. Penn State has sort of been on the periphery as my uncle and cousins went there. I even got accepted to Penn State, but chose Drexel instead.

I never really have chosen a college football team to follow, cheer for, and watch. Lehigh is in the Patriot league and is no match for any of the teams you see on ESPN or national television. Penn State is obviously in the Big Ten Conference, would be on national TV, so I feel like I should be inclined to cheer for them as they're in my home state.

But I'm going to open it up to the internet. What team should I watch this year? What school is going to be interesting and fun to watch? Let me know with some reasons why.
