I keep hearing people talking about [John Steinbeck's East of Eden](https://bookshop.org/p/books/east-of-eden-john-steinbeck/16287413?ean=9780140186390). I keep hearing lots of praise about it, and I keep thinking about reading it earlier this year and having to at times force myself to finish it.
<!-- excerpt -->

One of the things I've tried to do this year has been mix in classics in my reading. I read [Frankenstein](https://bookshop.org/p/books/frankenstein-mary-shelley/6a5ab762f4a4486e) by Mary Shelley back in March and chose East of Eden in June as the 2nd in my classics in 2026 reading. I enjoyed Frankenstein. I did not enjoy East of Eden and for some reason I keep re-visiting it wondering if I missed something in the story that would've made a difference. I don't know whether it's because it's a classic and you're "supposed" to like the classics, but for whatever reason, I haven't been able to convince myself it was worth liking.

I don't write this to try to dissuade anyone from reading it. I'd encourage it even as it is a classic and hopefully you'll get something more out of it than I did.

There's always the [Netflix Mini Series](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt21097264/) (which I intend on watching).
