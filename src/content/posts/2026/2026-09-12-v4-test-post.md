My wife and I recently did a re-watch (for me) of the HBO series *[Band of Brothers](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0185906/)*. It tells the story of Easy Company of the 101st Airborne in World War II. It's based on true stories and real people and I can't say enough good things about it. It was a fantastic miniseries that I would highly recommend to anyone.
<!-- excerpt -->

## The Series

I originally watched *Band of Brothers* in 2023 after putting it off for too long of a time. I loved the series then as well. I wanted to do a re-watch of it after reading [Inferno](https://bookshop.org/p/books/inferno-the-world-at-war-1939-1945-max-hastings/15283304) and [Armageddon](https://bookshop.org/p/books/armageddon-the-battle-for-germany-1944-1945-max-hastings/8519135) by Sir Max Hastings. Easy Company was mentioned a few times in one or both of the books so I wanted to re-visit the series, as well as introduce my wife to it.

We watched all the episodes and I was again blown away by how well the series was done. The acting, the writing and directing were all top-notch. I also appreciate how they don't shy away from the hell that World War II was, and war as a whole, still is and will always be. The series does a good job of recognizing and honoring what the soldiers went through, while also keeping true to the story, hell, suffering, and all.

## The Podcast

There was a companion podcast released in 2021 that I decided to listen to this time around. It was actually really enlightening. They had writers and some of the actors on talking about how they wrote the episodes, how they prepared to take on the role of characters who in some cases felt larger than life. How everyone talked to the soldiers if they were still alive at the time, and their friends, families and loved ones. Hearing about how much work went into it to try to stay true to history was impressive and I would highly recommend listening to it, especially as you watch the episodes.
